Privacy Policy

Fanfic Studio · Effective 27 August 2026

Fanfic Studio is an AI-assisted creative writing app. This policy explains exactly what the app collects, why, and who it is shared with. We do not run advertising, we do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not sell your data.

What we collect

DataWhy
Email address and account identifier, received from Sign in with Apple or Google Sign-InTo create and authenticate your account. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email", we only ever see the relay address.
Display name and language preferenceTo show your name on stories you publish and to generate text in your chosen language.
Stories you create: titles, settings, character descriptions, plot prompts, tags, and the generated chapter textTo store your work and to display stories you choose to publish.
Purchase records: Apple transaction identifiers, product identifier, chapter quantity, amount and currencyTo credit purchased chapters to your balance and to honour refunds. We never receive your card details — payment is handled entirely by Apple.
Interaction counts: likes and view counts on stories, and reports you submitTo rank the public catalogue and to moderate reported content.

The app requests no access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or any other device capability, and it does not use the App Tracking Transparency framework because it does not track.

Who processes your data

  • Supabase — hosts the database, authentication, and server functions. All account and story data is stored here.
  • OpenRouter — routes chapter generation requests to a large language model. The prompt we send contains only the story details you entered and the previous chapters of that story. It does not contain your email, name, or account identifier.
  • Apple — provides Sign in with Apple and processes all in-app purchases.
  • Google — provides Google Sign-In, if you choose that method.

We disclose data to no one else, except where we are legally required to.

Content you publish

Stories are published to the public catalogue by default, where any user of the app can read them alongside your display name. You can delete any story or individual chapter from the app at any time, which removes it from the catalogue and from our database.

Deleting your account

Settings → Delete Account permanently removes your profile, every story and chapter you wrote, your likes, your reports, and your authentication record. The deletion cascades immediately and is not recoverable. Purchase records required for tax and refund obligations may be retained in anonymised form with no link to you.

Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. Once you delete your account, the data is gone — we do not operate a grace period or a soft-delete archive.

Children

Fanfic Studio is rated 12+ and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

You can access and export your stories from within the app, correct your display name in Settings, and erase everything via Delete Account. If you are in the EEA or the UK, you may also contact us to exercise your rights under the GDPR, including the right to lodge a complaint with your supervisory authority. Our legal basis for processing is performance of our contract with you (running the app) and, for moderation, our legitimate interest in keeping the catalogue safe.

Changes

If we change this policy materially, we will update the effective date above and note the change in the app before it takes effect.
